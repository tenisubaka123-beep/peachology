## 💰 Program Pricing & Plans

All prices are clearly labeled as **one-time program fees** with **total payable price and applicable VAT included**:

1. **Free Trial — €0**
   - *Included:* Initial fund check and one introductory class
   - *Fee Type:* One-time program fee
   - *Total Payable:* **€0** (100% Free)
2. **Standard — €299 / 8 weeks (Main Launch Price)**
   - *Status:* **Recommended Main Launch Price** (to validate demand and delivery costs)
   - *Included:* Four modules, eight weekly group classes, materials, feedback on four module assignments, and a final presentation
   - *Fee Type:* One-time program fee
   - *Total Payable:* **€299.00** *(Net €249.17 + 20% VAT €49.83)*
3. **Individual Mentoring — €549 / 8 weeks**
   - *Included:* Everything in Standard, plus four 45-minute individual mentoring sessions
   - *Fee Type:* One-time program fee
   - *Total Payable:* **€549.00** *(Net €457.50 + 20% VAT €91.50)*

> **Pricing Policy Notice:** Proposed launch prices, subject to checking delivery costs and VAT treatment in launch countries. Launching with €299 as the main price to validate demand before adjusting.

## 🇪🇺 European Fund Check Clarification

Before publishing, European student founders need to understand exactly what is assessed and what they receive:

### 1. What is assessed during the check:
- **Student & Graduation Timing:** University enrollment or graduation within the last 12–24 months (under-28 youth entrepreneurship schemes).
- **Legal Structure & Location:** Pre-incorporation stage, micro-entreprise, or registered SAS/SARL in France or EU.
- **Sector & Sustainability Alignment:** Regional innovation, youth commerce, eco-transition retail, circular economy.
- **Capital Need vs. Non-Dilutive Caps:** Matching target seed need (€10k–€100k) against regional subsidies and 0% honor loan ratios.

### 2. What you receive immediately:
- **Verified Match Report:** Fictional demonstration matches based on authentic French & European policies (e.g. Paris Tremplin Jeunes Grant, 0% Prêt d'Honneur, Green Retail Subsidies).
- **Eligibility Verification Checklist:** Specific criteria to check before official dossier submission.
- **Provisional Financial Action Plan:** Direct link into the 12-Month Runway Planner.
- **Zero-Obligation Guarantee:** 100% free, no credit card or account barrier.
